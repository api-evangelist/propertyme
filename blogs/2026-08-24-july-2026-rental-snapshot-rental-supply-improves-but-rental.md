Australia’s rental market remained tight in July, with the national vacancy rate holding steady at 1.3%, according to SQM Research. While the number of available rental properties increased over the month, national asking rents continued to rise, sitting 7.2% higher than this time last year. The national figures only tell part of the story, however.
