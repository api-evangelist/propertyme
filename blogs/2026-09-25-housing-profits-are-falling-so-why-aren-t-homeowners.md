Australia’s housing market is starting to look different after several years of strong price growth. The share of profitable property resales fell to 95.4% in the June quarter, down from a 21-year high of 96.1% in March, Cotality’s latest Pain & Gain report shows. The median gain also fell from $378,000 to $371,000.
