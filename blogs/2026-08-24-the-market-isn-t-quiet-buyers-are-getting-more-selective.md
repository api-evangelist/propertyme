With higher interest rates, affordability pressures and ongoing market uncertainty, it’s easy to assume buyers are simply stepping back. But look a little closer and a different picture emerges. Buyers are still looking, but they’re becoming more considered about where they look, what they’re prepared to pay and when they’re willing to make a move.
