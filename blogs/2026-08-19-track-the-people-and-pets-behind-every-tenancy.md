When it’s time to renew a lease, you shouldn’t have to dig through notes, files or old messages to find out who’s approved to live at the property or whether there’s an extra set of paws, wings or fins to account for. Because pets are part of the picture too. With PropertyMe, you can now record approved occupants and pet details directly on the tenancy.
