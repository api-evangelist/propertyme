Most Sales Agents and Business Development Managers (BDMs) know they should follow up. The problem is that too much follow-up still sounds the same: “Just checking in to see if you’ve had a chance to think about it.” It’s easy to ignore because there’s nothing new to respond to. Good follow-up isn’t about sending more messages.
